Is GTA 6 coming to PC? The expected timeline

Last updated July 17, 2026

GTA 6 is not coming to PC at launch. It releases November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC version is announced — but Rockstar has eventually brought every modern GTA to PC, and its track record points to late 2027–2028.

Key takeaways

  • No PC version at launch and no announced date.
  • Precedent: GTA V hit PC ~19 months after console; RDR2 took ~13 months.
  • A reasonable expectation is late 2027 to 2028 — unconfirmed.
  • The PC launch is historically a second revenue wave: double-dip sales, mods, and a fresh content cycle.

The delay is strategy, not neglect: console-first limits piracy during the critical first-year window, simplifies optimization to two hardware targets, and converts the most impatient PC players into double-dip buyers who purchase twice.

Why the PC release matters financially

The PC wave is a second launch: returning buyers, the modding ecosystem, and — if it lands alongside a major online expansion — a second hype cycle for TTWO. For content creators, PC also unlocks the mod-showcase genre that fueled years of GTA V content.

How to prepare without wasting money

Don’t buy hardware for unpublished specs. If you’re building anyway, the console baseline implies a fast NVMe SSD and a mid-range-or-better GPU will be the realistic floor. Otherwise: wait for Rockstar’s official requirements, and subscribe to alerts on the command center — we’ll ping you when the PC announcement lands.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. No PC version has been announced, but Rockstar has brought every modern GTA to PC eventually.

When will GTA 6 release on PC?

Unannounced. Based on Rockstar’s pattern — GTA V took ~19 months, RDR2 ~13 months — a reasonable expectation is late 2027 to 2028. Treat any firm date you see as speculation.

Should I buy a console or wait for PC?

If you need day-one access (creators especially), console is the only option. If you mainly play on PC and can wait a year or more, history says a technically superior PC version arrives — often alongside a second content wave.

What PC specs will GTA 6 need?

Unknown until Rockstar publishes requirements. The console baseline (RDNA 2-class GPU, fast NVMe SSD, 16GB shared memory) suggests a mid-range gaming PC of 2026-27 with a fast SSD will be the realistic floor.
